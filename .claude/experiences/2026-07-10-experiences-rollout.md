# Experiences decision log added to this repo

- **Status** - shipped. The experiences folder was created and wired into
  the root agent instructions as a standing convention.
- **Context** - rolled out 2026-07-10 across every workspace repo, from
  the pattern proven in another private repo since 2026-06-30, where it
  stopped settled decisions (palette, deploy workflow, rejected SEO
  schemes) from being relitigated by later sessions.
- **The why, as given** - "memory already remembers what we did but this
  will really drill in why we did it and how we wish to handle current
  changes when compared to past requirements and our learnings from it."
  Git history records the what; this folder records the reasoning, so a
  change like removing a feature carries its cost and pain points with it
  instead of losing them when the session ends.
- **Decision** - three standing rules, recorded in the root agent
  instructions: consult experiences before big decisions; when asked to
  do or undo something significant without a stated reason, ask why and
  record the answer; append a dated entry after any non-trivial decision
  or significant agent-run experiment. Entries can be written by humans
  or auto-generated by agents, but the why must be real, not
  reconstructed after the fact.
- **Revisit if** - entries stop being written (the folder rots), or the
  index grows past skimmability, at which point tooling or pruning is
  worth a look.
