# Code of conduct

This project is a place for people to learn, build, and review Cloudflare automation safely.
Treat other participants with respect, including when you disagree about a technical choice.

## Expected behavior

- Give feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent while still asking for evidence.
- Respect stated boundaries and different levels of experience.
- Protect private account data. Do not post tokens, raw snapshots, private email addresses, or
  other people's identifying information.
- Accept a maintainer's decision to pause or move a discussion when it becomes unproductive.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual attention, deliberate exposure of private data, and
repeated personal attacks are not accepted.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct problems privately to
[hello@abhiigatty.com](mailto:hello@abhiigatty.com). Include links or screenshots where useful,
but do not send Cloudflare tokens or raw account data.

The maintainer will review reports privately and may remove content, lock a discussion, issue a
warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports involving the maintainer
can be sent through GitHub's abuse-reporting tools.

This code applies to the repository, its issue and pull-request discussions, and project spaces
run by the maintainer.
